What Worked Well
Inbound call leads convert at ~50% CL2A — half of everyone who calls in books an appointment. Website and Meta/WhatsApp together are ~24% of leads but delivering an outsized share of appointments. These three sources combined are your quality pipeline — protect their volume and routing priority.
⚠️
What Needs Improvement
Agent Submission is 35% of all leads at ~1.9% CL2A. That means 35% of your caller capacity is being spent on leads that convert at 1 in 50. The opportunity cost is enormous — those call slots could go to website or Meta leads that convert at 35–40%. Source data quality is also broken: 392 raw values, same source in multiple spellings. You cannot reliably score or route by source until this is cleaned. A single normalisation field in the lead ingestion flow would fix this entirely.
