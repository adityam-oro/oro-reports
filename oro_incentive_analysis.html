Summary

What changed after Sep 2025

Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Pune moved to scheme-based payouts in Sep 2025. Vijayawada retained its original structure. Guntur, Warangal and Karimnagar launched in Oct 2025 on a higher new-city incentive structure.

Partners joining after Aug 2025 are excluded from H1 figures. Salary = monthly CTC × active months. H1 = Jan–Aug 2025 · H2 = Sep 2025–Apr 2026.
How to read the Change columns bps (basis points) — for % of ND. 1 bps = 0.01 pp. −11 bps means incentive cost fell from 0.27% to 0.16% per ₹ disbursed.   pp (percentage points) — for % of salary. Arithmetic difference. −7 pp means for every ₹100 in salary, incentive went from ₹47 to ₹40. Negative = better for the business in both cases.
