03 · Effort × quality — the four quadrants

Each dot is one SP — totals summed across the selected months, then rescored, same as the leaderboard below (so an SP appears in exactly one quadrant, never split across quadrants when multiple months are selected). Hover a dot for the SP's name. Vertical = effort (daily-capped Cx Met + leads÷2, vs 6/day). Horizontal = quality (visit % vs 40% target, blended with loan rate vs 80%). Lines at 50% of each. A visit-raised floor overrides the quadrant: any SP with visit-raised % below 35% is classified At Risk regardless of effort or quality — high effort does not offset a visit-raised rate this low. Its dot is plotted inside the At-risk box itself (not at its raw effort/quality position), so a dot's box always matches its color.

SP scoped to your current-active roster (Employee ID list) matched via the Sales Table's Emp_id column, restricted to ≥ 6 months tenure — 50 of 61 active, Tier-1-city SPs qualify; 11 are excluded for being under 6 months.

Data: identity, city, active/inactive status, tenure (Working Months), Cx Met, Visits Raised, and Loan Completed all come from the "Latest Sales Table - July 22" workbook (41,259 visit-level rows), cross-checked against the current-active Employee ID roster you supplied. Cx Met and Visits Raised count every visit for the SP regardless of lead source. Leads submitted = self-sourced leads only, from Quali-prod.lead: a lead counts if created_by_oro_id matches the SP AND lead_source is their own @orocorp.in email, OR appointment_booked_by exactly matches their name — verified against your dashboard's city/month totals (broad-vs-self-sourced definitions differ by roughly 2x; self-sourced is the narrower, correct one). Effort is capped per day at 6 units (min(met + leads÷2, 6)) before being summed into the month, so a burst of leads on one day can no longer push a whole month over 100%. Scope: active SPs with ≥ 6 months tenure only, Bengaluru/Hyderabad/Pune only, Jan–Jul 2026 (Jul is MTD through 22 Jul). Working days = calendar days minus Sundays only (no holiday calendar). Chennai is excluded from this rubric — Chennai SPs don't receive company-provided appointments, so this effort/quality model doesn't fit their work and needs its own rule set.

Loan completion note: the workbook's C- Loan Completed checkbox is stale — org-wide it is set on only ~18% of visits that already have a real loan account id (loan_number, formatted like OMGL41596102) attached, and for July it was blank on all 145 such visits despite loans clearly having been booked. Loan completion in this report is therefore counted directly off loan_number: a visit counts as a completed loan if it reached Visits Raised status (VISIT_COMPLETED_BRL / VISIT_COMPLETED_GL / VISIT_CANCELLED_CC) AND has a non-blank loan_number, matched via the workbook's Agent Auth ID and Appointment Month columns. This raised the roster-wide loan count from 375 to 2,019 across Jan–Jul, and from 0 to 77 for July MTD.
