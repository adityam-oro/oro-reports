01 · Rules of scoring
MetricDefinitionTarget
Cx MetEvery visit for the SP (any lead source — not restricted to self-sourced leads) whose status reaches: VISIT_COMPLETED_GL, VISIT_COMPLETED_BRL, VISIT_COMPLETED_GBS, MAY_TXN, VISIT_CANCELLED_NIAM, VISIT_CANCELLED_IE, VISIT_CANCELLED_GS, MAY_TXN_RESCHEDULED, MAY_TXN_RNR, VISIT_CANCELLED_CC5 / day
Callable leads — newSelf-sourced only: leads where submitted_by matches the SP's own agent id in Quali-prod.lead_submissions, counted once per distinct lead (deduped by lead_id), where the lead was ever accepted2 leads = 1 Cx Met
Callable leads — existingSame source, but the lead was never accepted (a resubmission of an already-known lead) — counts at half weight, so repeatedly resubmitting the same lead can't inflate effort the way a genuinely new lead does4 leads = 1 Cx Met
Daily capPer SP, per calendar day: min(Cx Met that day + weighted leads that day ÷ 2, 5), where weighted leads = new leads + existing leads × 0.5. A day cannot contribute more than 5 units no matter how many leads are submitted — this stops a burst of low-effort lead submissions from inflating the monthly effort score≤ 5 / day, then summed for the month
Effort scoreSum of daily-capped units for the month ÷ (5 × working days)≥ 50% clears the effort bar
Visits raisedVisit reaches status: VISIT_COMPLETED_BRL, VISIT_COMPLETED_GL, VISIT_CANCELLED_CC40% of Cx Met
Loan completionVisit reached Visits Raised status AND its linked lead has a conversion_id pointing to a real loan (joined live: Quali-prod.sales_visit → lead.conversion_id → Oro.loans), ÷ Visits Raised.80% of visits raised
Quality scoreAverage of (visit % ÷ 40%) and (loan rate ÷ 80%), each capped at 100% before averaging so quality can never read above 100%≥ 80% clears the quality bar

Scope: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune only, all currently-active SPs — no minimum tenure requirement. Chennai is excluded — its SPs don't receive company-provided appointments, so this rubric's effort/quality definitions don't apply and a separate rule set is needed for that city. Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month minus that city's state bank holidays (2026 Federal Bank calendar) landing on an otherwise-working day — so working days can differ slightly by city. The current month is month-to-date. There is no floor on visit-raised % — a low visit-raised rate is reflected through the quality score itself, not a separate override.

Data source: refreshed automatically every morning, live from Metabase — Cx Met, Visits Raised, and Loan Completion come from Quali-prod.sales_visit (status codes) joined to lead.conversion_id and the production loans table (for loan completion), and self-sourced leads come from Quali-prod.lead_submissions where submitted_by matches the SP, deduped by lead_id (a lead resubmitted multiple times counts once, attributed to its first submission). Cx Met and Visits Raised count every visit for the SP regardless of lead source; leads submitted are self-sourced only, and count at full weight if ever accepted, half weight if never accepted (a resubmission of an already-known lead) — the same accept/reject-weighted logic used on the AP report. Effort is capped per day at 5 units (min(met + weighted_leads÷2, 5)) before being summed into the month, so a burst of leads on one day can no longer push a whole month over 100%. Scope: active SPs, Bengaluru/Hyderabad/Pune only (no minimum tenure requirement), Jan 2026 to present (the current month is MTD). Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month minus that city's state bank holidays. Chennai is excluded from this rubric — Chennai SPs don't receive company-provided appointments, so this effort/quality model doesn't fit their work and needs its own rule set.
