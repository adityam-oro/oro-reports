02 · Org view — month over month

Each dot is one SP — totals summed across the selected months, then rescored, same as the leaderboard below (so an SP appears in exactly one quadrant, never split across quadrants when multiple months are selected). Hover a dot for the SP's name. The four boxes are always an even 50/50 grid — a dot's box is its actual assigned zone (which already accounts for the effort bar, the 80% quality bar, the 35% visit-raised floor, and the lead-conversion override below), not a direct plot of raw effort/quality against those thresholds. Position within a box is ranked by that SP's real effort (vertical) and quality (horizontal) among others in the same zone, so relative standing inside a quadrant still means something. A visit-raised floor overrides the quadrant: any SP with visit-raised % below 35% is classified At Risk regardless of effort or quality. A lead-quality override also applies: any SP with effort ≥50% whose self-sourced leads convert to loans at under 2% is classified Coaching even if their visit-based quality clears 80%, since the leads behind their effort score aren't good ones.

Rows highlighted gold are SPs within their first 3 months of joining; rows highlighted blue are At risk AND within their first 3 months — held out of every stat and chart above, but still shown here so nobody drops out of sight entirely.

Data source: refreshed automatically every morning, live from Metabase — Cx Met, Visits Raised, and Loan Completion come from Quali-prod.sales_visit (status codes) joined to lead.conversion_id and the production loans table (for loan completion), and self-sourced leads come from Quali-prod.lead_submissions where submitted_by matches the SP, deduped by lead_id (a lead resubmitted multiple times counts once, attributed to its first submission). Cx Met and Visits Raised count every visit for the SP regardless of lead source; leads submitted are self-sourced only, and count at full weight if ever accepted, half weight if never accepted (a resubmission of an already-known lead) — the same accept/reject-weighted logic used on the AP report. Effort is capped per day at 6 units (min(met + weighted_leads÷2, 6)) before being summed into the month, so a burst of leads on one day can no longer push a whole month over 100%. Lead→loan conversion tracks, separately from visit-based quality, whether each SP's distinct submitted leads ever resulted in a lead.conversion_id — i.e. whether the leads behind their effort score are actually good leads. Scope: active SPs, Bengaluru/Hyderabad/Pune only (no minimum tenure requirement), Jan 2026 to present (the current month is MTD). Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month minus that city's state bank holidays. Chennai is excluded from this rubric — Chennai SPs don't receive company-provided appointments, so this effort/quality model doesn't fit their work and needs its own rule set. New-joiner exception: At-risk SPs within their first 3 months (measured against the end of the selected window, not today's date) are excluded from every stat and chart but shown, highlighted blue, in the leaderboard; others within 3 months are shown and highlighted gold.
