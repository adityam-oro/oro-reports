01 · Rules of scoring
MetricDefinitionTarget
Cx MetEvery visit for the SP (any lead source — not restricted to self-sourced leads) whose status reaches: VISIT_COMPLETED_GL, VISIT_COMPLETED_BRL, MAY_TXN, VISIT_CANCELLED_NIAM, VISIT_CANCELLED_IE, VISIT_CANCELLED_GS, MAY_TXN_RESCHEDULED, MAY_TXN_RNR, VISIT_CANCELLED_CC6 / day
Callable leadsSelf-sourced only: leads where created_by_oro_id matches the SP's own agent id in Quali-prod.lead.2 leads = 1 Cx Met
Daily capPer SP, per calendar day: min(Cx Met that day + leads that day ÷ 2, 6). A day cannot contribute more than 6 units no matter how many leads are submitted — this stops a burst of low-effort lead submissions from inflating the monthly effort score≤ 6 / day, then summed for the month
Effort scoreSum of daily-capped units for the month ÷ (6 × working days)≥ 50% clears the effort bar
Visits raisedVisit reaches status: VISIT_COMPLETED_BRL, VISIT_COMPLETED_GL, VISIT_CANCELLED_CC40% of Cx Met
Loan completionVisit reached Visits Raised status AND its linked lead has a conversion_id pointing to a real loan (joined live: Quali-prod.sales_visit → lead.conversion_id → Oro.loans), ÷ Visits Raised.80% of visits raised
Quality scoreAverage of (visit % ÷ 40%) and (loan rate ÷ 80%), each capped at 100% before averaging so quality can never read above 100%≥ 50% clears the quality bar

Scope: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune only, all currently-active SPs — no minimum tenure requirement. Chennai is excluded — its SPs don't receive company-provided appointments, so this rubric's effort/quality definitions don't apply and a separate rule set is needed for that city. Working days = calendar days minus Sundays (no holiday calendar available). The current month is month-to-date.

03 · Effort × quality — the four quadrants

Each dot is one SP — totals summed across the selected months, then rescored, same as the leaderboard below (so an SP appears in exactly one quadrant, never split across quadrants when multiple months are selected). Hover a dot for the SP's name. Vertical = effort (daily-capped Cx Met + leads÷2, vs 6/day). Horizontal = quality (visit % vs 40% target, blended with loan rate vs 80%). Lines at 50% of each. A visit-raised floor overrides the quadrant: any SP with visit-raised % below 35% is classified At Risk regardless of effort or quality — high effort does not offset a visit-raised rate this low. Its dot is plotted inside the At-risk box itself (not at its raw effort/quality position), so a dot's box always matches its color.

At risk — below both, or visit-raised % under 35% floor Coaching — effort ok, quality short Push volume — quality ok, effort short Star — clears both
