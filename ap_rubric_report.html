Objective: 100 points/day. Points/day is capped at 100 — a day (approximated at the monthly-average level: total points ÷ working days) can never contribute more than 100, capped before summing across multiple selected months, so a stronger month can't be blended with a weaker one to dodge the cap. Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month (Oro's actual off-day policy) minus that AP's city's state bank holidays (2026 Federal Bank calendar) that land on an otherwise-working day — so working days can differ slightly by city. The current month is month-to-date. Bands (on the capped 0–100 scale): below 60 is At risk; 60–75 needs improvement; 75–95 good performance; above 95 is a star performer. Scope: all active Appraisal Partners / Senior Appraisal Partners / Appraisal Leads / Appraisal Trainees, all cities — except APs who are both At risk and still within their first 3 months of joining, who are held out of this view (those who remain within their first 3 months but aren't At risk are shown, highlighted, in the leaderboard). Cx escalations and recovery-case handling are tracked separately and are not yet incorporated into this score — noted here for future inclusion.

Lead bonus (not part of the core score)ValueDefinition
Lead bonus+10Added to the AP's final points/day, after the 100pt/day cap (so it can push the final score above 100), if over the selected months the AP both submitted an average of 100+ distinct leads per month and had 2%+ of those distinct leads convert to a loan. Lead generation itself earns no points toward the core score — this bonus is the only way lead activity affects the score

A distinct lead is counted once per lead_id (lead_submissions.submitted_by), whether it was ever accepted (New leads) or never accepted — i.e. a resubmission of an already-known lead (Existing leads). Both count toward the volume floor; the conversion rate is converted leads ÷ total distinct leads (New + Existing). "Converted" means lead.conversion_id is set (the lead resulted in a loan).
