Objective: 100 points/day. Points/day is capped at 100 — a day (approximated at the monthly-average level: total points ÷ working days) can never contribute more than 100, capped before summing across multiple selected months, so a stronger month can't be blended with a weaker one to dodge the cap. Lead Generation has no separate daily cap. Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month (Oro's actual off-day policy) minus that AP's city's state bank holidays (2026 Federal Bank calendar) that land on an otherwise-working day — so working days can differ slightly by city. The current month is month-to-date. Bands (on the capped 0–100 scale): below 60 is At risk; 60–75 needs improvement; 75–95 good performance; above 95 is a star performer. Scope: all active Appraisal Partners / Senior Appraisal Partners / Appraisal Leads / Appraisal Trainees, all cities — except APs who are both At risk and still within their first 3 months of joining, who are held out of this view (those who remain within their first 3 months but aren't At risk are shown, highlighted, in the leaderboard). Cx escalations and recovery-case handling are tracked separately and are not yet incorporated into this score — noted here for future inclusion.

02 · Org view — month over month

Each dot is one AP — totals summed across the selected months, then rescored, same as the leaderboard. Hover a dot for the AP's name. Quadrant is driven by band: bottom-left At risk, bottom-right Needs improvement, top-left Good performance, top-right Star performer. Since the AP rubric is a single score (not effort × quality like the SP rubric), a dot's position within its quadrant is arbitrary jitter (not a second metric) — only which quadrant it's in is meaningful.
