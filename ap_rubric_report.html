Goal: 100 points a day. We add up your points for the months picked above, divide by your working days, and cap it at 100/day so one great month can't hide a bad one. Bands: below 60 At risk, 60–75 Needs improvement, 75–95 Good, above 95 Star. Covers Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune.

If you raise/source a visit yourself (a referral or self-sourced customer) and someone else conducts and completes it, you get a flat 30-point raiser bonus, on top of whatever they earn for the completion — this applies no matter the loan type. If you raise a visit and it's cancelled before it completes, you get nothing for the raise. If you raise a visit AND complete it yourself, you just get the normal completion points above — the raiser bonus doesn't stack with your own completion.

Lead bonusRule
VolumeSend 100+ leads a month, on average.
Quality2%+ of those leads turn into a loan.
Bonus+10 points — only if both rules above are true at the same time.

A lead is any customer lead you send us, from any source, counted once. "Converted" means it became a loan. This does not include loans you close yourself by doing the appraisal visit — those are already scored above.

Why these numbers: 100/month is a real bar — no partner outside Bengaluru has ever hit it. 2% is the point where Bengaluru partners split into two groups — good leads vs. just a lot of leads. Partners in the other three cities are almost always above 2% anyway, so this rule mostly matters for Bengaluru.

Points/day is the capped-at-100 core daily average plus the +10 Lead Bonus if earned (so it can read above 100) — this combined figure is also what's used for banding. Rows highlighted gold are APs within their first 3 months of joining; rows highlighted blue are At risk AND within their first 3 months — held out of every stat and chart above, but still shown here so nobody drops out of sight entirely.

Data source: live from Metabase (Oro 2.0 visits/gbs_visits, Quali-prod lead/lead_submissions). Lead generation does not feed the core score directly — it only counts each distinct submitted lead once (deduped by lead_id, not by submission attempt, across all lead origins) toward the +10 Lead Bonus, which requires an average of 100+ distinct leads/month and 2%+ of them converting to a loan, over the selected window. New-joiner exception: At-risk APs within their first 3 months (measured against the end of the selected window, not today's date) are excluded from every stat and chart but shown, highlighted blue, in the leaderboard; others within 3 months are shown and highlighted gold.
