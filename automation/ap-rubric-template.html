Objective: 100 points/day. Points/day is capped at 100 — a day (approximated at the monthly-average level: total points ÷ working days) can never contribute more than 100, capped before summing across multiple selected months, so a stronger month can't be blended with a weaker one to dodge the cap. Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus holidays (holidays assumed at 6/month, or the actual count of known government holidays falling on a working day if that's higher). The current month is month-to-date. Bands (on the capped 0–100 scale): below 50 is At risk; 50–70 needs improvement; 70–90 good performance; above 90 is a star performer. Scope: all active Appraisal Partners / Senior Appraisal Partners / Appraisal Leads / Appraisal Trainees, all cities — except APs who are both At risk and still within their first 3 months of joining, who are held out of this view (those who remain within their first 3 months but aren't At risk are shown, highlighted, in the leaderboard). Cx escalations and recovery-case handling are tracked separately and are not yet incorporated into this score — noted here for future inclusion.

Data source: refreshed live from Metabase — completion/cancellation events come from Oro 2.0's visits table. Fresh Loan / Takeover are identified by loan_subtype; Release / Private sale are identified by visit_type = 'GR' (verified 2026-07-28: release_type is populated on barely 2% of real release completions, and the RELEASE_VISIT_COMPLETED status itself was only adopted in July 2026 — before that, releases carry ordinary VISIT_COMPLETED status, so visit_type='GR' is the only reliable signal across the full date range; this matches the org-wide ~1,200–1,600 releases/month baseline). Gold sale comes from gbs_visits; Lead Generation / Self-sourced Cx Met come from Quali-prod's lead and sales_visit tables (a lead counts as self-sourced when lead_source and appointment_booked_by both match the AP themselves). Scope: active Appraisal Partners / Senior Appraisal Partners / Appraisal Leads / Appraisal Trainees, all cities, Jan 2026 to present (current month is MTD, through 28 July). Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus holidays (6/month assumed, or the actual count of known government holidays landing on a working day if higher). Points/day is capped at 100 (approximated at the monthly-average level — true day-level capping would need day-level data not currently pulled). Cx escalations and recovery-case handling are not yet scored — pending a defined point value.

Self-sourced Cx Met is scored only where a self-sourced lead traces through to a completed appraisal visit via the sales_visit table — most self-sourced AP leads don't flow through that intermediate table (it's primarily the Sales Partner scheduling layer), so this figure is a lower bound, not a complete count.

New-joiner exception: an AP who is At risk and still within their first 3 months of joining (by date of joining) is held out of every table and stat on this page — they're excluded entirely, not just re-labeled — until either they clear 3 months' tenure or their score improves.

Roster note: 91 of 135 currently-active APs (per the 2026-07-28 HR sheet) are represented here — the other 44 (mostly late-2025/2026 joiners, or people promoted into management roles) could not yet be resolved to their in-app agent id and will be added once resolved.
