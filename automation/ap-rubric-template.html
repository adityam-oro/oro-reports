Objective: 100 points/day. Points/day is capped at 100 — a day (approximated at the monthly-average level: total points ÷ working days) can never contribute more than 100, capped before summing across multiple selected months, so a stronger month can't be blended with a weaker one to dodge the cap. Working days = calendar days minus Sundays minus the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month (Oro's actual off-day policy) minus that AP's city's state bank holidays (2026 Federal Bank calendar) that land on an otherwise-working day — so working days can differ slightly by city. The current month is month-to-date. Bands (on the capped 0–100 scale): below 60 is At risk; 60–75 needs improvement; 75–95 good performance; above 95 is a star performer. A distinct lead is counted once per lead_id (lead_submissions.submitted_by), whether it was ever accepted (New leads) or never accepted — i.e. a resubmission of an already-known lead (Existing leads). Both count toward the volume floor; the conversion rate is converted leads ÷ total distinct leads (New + Existing). "Converted" means lead.conversion_id is set (the lead resulted in a loan). Lead counts include all lead origins (partner app, customer app, and others) per the user's instruction — not filtered by source channel.

01b · Lead bonus — rules explained

The Lead Bonus is the only way sending leads affects your score. Here is everything you need to know about it, in plain language.

TermWhat it means
A leadAny customer lead you send us. It can come from any source — the partner app, the customer app, or anywhere else. We do not care where it came from, only that you sent it.
Counted onceEach lead is counted only one time, even if you send the same lead more than once. Sending it again does not give you extra credit.
New leadA lead that we accepted the first time it came in. This means it was a genuinely new customer we did not already have.
Existing leadA lead that was never accepted, meaning it's a customer we already had in our system before you sent it. It still gets counted as a lead you sent, but it is not a new customer.
Converted leadA lead that went all the way and turned into an actual loan.
Conversion %Converted leads ÷ total leads you sent, as a percentage. This shows how many of your leads are turning into real business, not just how many leads you send.
Bonus ruleWhat you need to do
Rule 1 — VolumeSend an average of 100 or more leads every month. We add up all your leads over the months you're being scored on, then divide by the number of months — even months where you sent nothing still count toward the average.
Rule 2 — QualityAt least 2 out of every 100 leads you send must turn into a loan (2%+ conversion).
The bonusBoth rules must be true at the same time. Sending a lot of leads with no conversions does not earn it. Having a great conversion rate on very few leads does not earn it either. You need both volume and quality together.
What you get+10 points added to your points/day above, on top of everything else you already earn.

What does NOT count: loans you close yourself by doing the appraisal visit (Fresh Loan, Takeover, and so on) are scored separately above — they are not part of the Lead Bonus. The Lead Bonus only looks at loans that came from a lead you personally sent us.

01c · Why 100 leads and 2%?

Why 100 leads a month? Because that is a real, hard number — not one picked out of thin air. Only partners in Bengaluru ever send this many leads. No partner in Chennai, Hyderabad, or Pune has hit this mark, not even once, not even in their best month. So 100 is the true bar that only the busiest partners can clear.

Why 2% conversion? We looked at how many leads turn into an actual loan, city by city, over four different time windows. The tables below show the count of partners in each conversion band.

In Chennai, Hyderabad, and Pune, almost every partner already sits above 2%. Moving the bar up or down barely changes anything there — most partners pass either way, so 2% is not really "testing" them.

Bengaluru is different. Its partners are spread out across every band — some near 0%, some well above 2%. That means 2% is the point where Bengaluru partners actually split into two clear groups: partners who send good leads, and partners who just send a lot of leads.

In short: 100 leads/month picks out the partners who send enough leads. 2% conversion then picks out, among them, the ones sending leads that actually turn into loans — instead of just leads for the sake of leads.
