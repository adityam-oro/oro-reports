01 · What this measures

Every distinct lead an AP submitted (lead_submissions.submitted_by, deduped by lead_id, all origins — partner app, customer app, and others), and what share of those specific leads went on to convert to a loan (lead.conversion_id set on that same lead). This is not the AP's total loan activity — Fresh Loan/Takeover completions from their own appraisal visits aren't counted here, only loans traced back to a lead they personally submitted.

Lead Bonus reference (from the AP Rubric report): +10 pts if an AP averages 100+ distinct leads/month and 2%+ of those leads convert, over the selected window — both required. "Avg leads/month" below is always total leads ÷ number of months selected, regardless of how many of those months had zero activity.
