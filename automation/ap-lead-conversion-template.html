01 · What this shows

How many leads each partner sent us, and how many of those leads turned into a loan. We count every lead the partner sent, from any source (partner app, customer app, or anything else). We count each lead only once, even if it was sent more than one time.

This is not the partner's total loan work. It does not include loans they closed by doing the appraisal visit themselves. It only counts loans that came from a lead they personally sent us.

Bonus rule (from the AP Rubric report): a partner gets +10 points if, on average, they send 100+ leads a month and 2%+ of those leads turn into a loan — both must be true. "Avg leads/month" is always total leads divided by the number of months picked above, even if some of those months had no activity at all.

04 · Why 100 leads and 2%?

To get the bonus, a partner needs both of these, at the same time:

1. They send us 100 leads or more, on average, every month.
2. At least 2 out of every 100 of those leads turn into a loan.

Why 100 leads a month?
Because that is a real, hard number — not one we picked out of thin air. Only partners in Bengaluru ever send this many leads. No partner in Chennai, Hyderabad, or Pune has hit this mark, not even once, not even in their best month. So 100 is the true bar that only the busiest partners can clear.

Why 2% conversion?
We looked at how many leads turn into an actual loan, city by city, over four different time windows. The tables below show the count of partners in each conversion band.

In Chennai, Hyderabad, and Pune, almost every partner already sits above 2%. Moving the bar up or down barely changes anything there — most partners pass either way, so 2% is not really "testing" them.

Bengaluru is different. Its partners are spread out across every band — some near 0%, some well above 2%. That means 2% is the point where Bengaluru partners actually split into two clear groups: partners who send good leads, and partners who just send a lot of leads.

In short: 100 leads/month picks out the partners who send enough leads. 2% conversion then picks out, among them, the ones sending leads that actually turn into loans — instead of just leads for the sake of leads. Together, the bonus only goes to partners doing both well.
